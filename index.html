Printing Guide

How to Measure Your Box

Always measure the internal dimensions (the space where your product sits), and provide dimensions in the following order:

  • Length — the longest side of the box opening
  • Width — the shorter side of the opening
  • Height — the depth from bottom to top edge

Please provide dimensions in L×W×H format, e.g. 24×22×4 cm. Not sure? Send us your product dimensions and we'll recommend the right box.

Printing Guide

What 1C / 4C / 0C Mean

Printing codes indicate the number of ink colors applied to each side of the box. The format is Outside + Inside.

0C No printing — blank/unprinted surface
1C Single color printing (1 color on one side)
4C Full color CMYK printing (4 colors)
4C+0C Full color outside, blank inside
4C+1C Full color outside, 1 color inside
4C+4C Full color both inside and outside

Other options available: Flexo 1C/2C, Brown Base, White Base, Laserfoil. Contact us for details.
