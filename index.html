Measurement Guide

How to Measure Your Box

Packaging box dimensions follow the international standard order of Length × Width × Height (L × W × H).

  • Length — the longest side of the box opening
  • Width — the shorter side of the opening
  • Height — the depth from bottom to top edge

Please provide dimensions in L×W×H format, e.g. 240×220×40 mm. Not sure? Send us your product dimensions and we'll recommend the right box.

Printing Guide

What 0C / 1C / 4C / 5C Mean

Printing codes indicate the number of ink colors applied to each side of the box. The format is Outside + Inside.

First — what each code means

0C No printing — that surface stays blank
1C One ink colour on one side — usually a logo or text
4C Full colour CMYK — four inks mix into any colour or photo
5C CMYK plus one spot colour — metallic, white or a Pantone colour

Every option in the Printing dropdown

Plain No printing outside or inside — the paper colour shows through
1C+0C 1 colour outside, blank inside
1C+1C 1 colour outside, 1 colour inside
4C+0C Full colour outside, blank inside
4C+1C Full colour outside, 1 colour inside
4C+4C Full colour outside and inside
5C+0C CMYK + 1 spot colour outside, blank inside
5C+1C CMYK + 1 spot colour outside, 1 colour inside
5C+4C CMYK + 1 spot colour outside, full colour inside
5C+5C CMYK + 1 spot colour on both sides — the fullest spec

Other options available: Flexo 1C/2C, Brown Base, White Base, Laserfoil. Not sure which to pick? Send us your artwork and we'll advise.

Finishing Guide

Lamination & Finishing Options

Surface finishing adds protection and enhances the look and feel of your packaging. Choose from the options below:

Matte Lamination

Smooth, non-reflective finish. Elegant and professional look.

Glossy Lamination

Shiny, reflective surface. Makes colors pop and vibrant.

Gloss Varnish

An effective dust-proof protective layer that gives packaging a practical, everyday shine.

Vacuum-Form Oil

A coating made for blister packaging, bonding the paper card to the plastic shell tightly once heated.

Spot UV

Glossy coating on specific areas for contrast effect.

Embossing

Raised design elements for a tactile, premium feel.

Foil Stamping

Metallic foil applied to specific areas for luxury effect.

Window Patch

A clear cut-out that shows the real product and its texture without opening the box.
